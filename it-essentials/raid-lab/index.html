RAID ≠ backup

RAID can provide redundancy but cannot replace separate backups. Deleted files, malware and disasters can still affect the array.

Teaching model

This simulator uses simplified four-disk RAID 5 and RAID 10 layouts. Real controllers, rebuild behavior and storage performance vary.
